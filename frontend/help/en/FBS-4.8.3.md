# Physical progress

## What the screen shows

The third card of the [project indicators](FBS-4.8.md), with its date of calculation: what share of the work promised is done, regardless of what it cost.

- **Physical progress**: the [earned value](notions/valeur-acquise.md) over the [reference budget](notions/budget-de-reference.md), in percent.
- The quantities it is deduced from: **Earned value** and **Reference budget**.

The earned value is obtained task by task, at completion, never by a percentage entered: the physical progress moves by steps, and its fineness is that of the division of the planning. A task started at 90 % of its time does not count yet. It is an assumed consequence of the method, not a flaw of calculation.

## Its commands

The card has no command: it is read, for the project or for the subproject the banner filters.

## Its columns and computed values

All the values are calculated by the server, without the provision lines; the tasks coming from an occurred risk earn nothing. The progress of each summary task, over its subtree, is read in the [planning grid](FBS-4.3.2.md).

## Keyboard gestures

The cards are read in the order of the page, before the charts.
