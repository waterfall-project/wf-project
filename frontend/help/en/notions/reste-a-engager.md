# Remaining to commit and updated estimate

## What the glossary says

> **Remaining to commit** — Designates the amount of the expenses remaining to be committed to complete the project.

> **Re-estimated amount** — Designates the amount of an estimate line as the last periodic review estimated it. It is on it that the remaining to commit is calculated.

> **Current estimate** — Designates the estimate of the main structure of the current revision of a project, provision lines included except when the text specifies “without provisions”. It is the one the pipeline sums for the projects in pricing.

> **Unanticipated line** — Designates an estimate line, or a task and its lines, added to the main structure after the reference revision for work that had not been costed. It bears a zero budgeted amount and weighs on the remaining to commit, never on the reference budget.

> **Projection at completion** — Designates the estimate of the total cost of the project at its end. Waterfall presents three of them, according to the hypothesis made on the remaining work.

## In Waterfall

As long as the project is in pricing, the estimate is entered in the [estimate](../FBS-4.4.md). As soon as it is In progress, the grid of the [remaining to commit](../FBS-4.5.md) becomes the updated estimate: that of the main structure is read only, and it is in the remaining to commit that the remaining expense is re-estimated, from the date of the review to the end of the project. A task or a line for work that had not been costed is added there: it is an unanticipated line.

The remaining to commit of a completed task is zero; that of a started task, or re-estimated, is the re-estimated amount of its lines; that of a task not started that no one has re-estimated is calculated from the quantities of the reference, on its current year of consumption. The provisions of the identified risks are added to it. The re-estimation is entered on the quantities — the effort, or the quantity and the disbursement —, and the amount follows; a zero remaining on all the lines of a task completes it, at a date asked at the validation.

The projection of the project manager is the sum of the actual cost and of the remaining to commit; the margin on the [reference budget](budget-de-reference.md) is this budget minus this projection, positive when some remains. The deviation from the previous review says by how much the review has moved the remaining to commit.
