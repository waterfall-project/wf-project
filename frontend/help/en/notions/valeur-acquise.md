# Earned value

## What the glossary says

> **Earned value** — Designates the value, at the budgeted price, of the work completed at a given date.

> **Physical progress** — Designates the share of the work promised that is done: the earned value over the reference budget.

## In Waterfall

The earned value measures what is done, at the price that had been promised: the work done counts for its budgeted amount, whatever it really cost. It is what makes it comparable both to the [planned value](valeur-planifiee.md) — has one done what was planned at that date? — and to the actual cost — has what is done cost what it should?

The earned value at a date is the sum of the budgeted amounts of the lines borne by the tasks completed at that date, without the provision lines, the date of completion of the task prevailing: a task earns its budget by completing, not before. The own lines of a summary task are earned when it is completed. An unanticipated line, of zero budgeted amount, earns nothing.

The physical progress is calculated for the project and by subproject, and for each summary task: the share of the budgeted amounts of its subtree already earned. It is this value that the column “Physical progress” of the planning grid shows.
