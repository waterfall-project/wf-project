# Planned value

## What the glossary says

> **Planned value** — Designates the share of the reference budget that should have been earned at a given date, according to the dates of the reference revision.

## In Waterfall

The planned value is the promise spread over time: at each date, the share of the [reference budget](budget-de-reference.md) that the planning of the reference planned to have achieved. It is drawn from the dates of the reference revision, never from those of the current revision: a planning that slips does not move the planned value, it widens the gap with it.

It is calculated on the lines counted in the reference budget, without the provision lines: each budgeted amount is spread over the duration of its task pro rata to the worked hours, according to the calendar of its role for a labour line, that of the task otherwise; a line borne by a milestone counts whole at the date of the milestone.

Like the budget, it moves only with a new reference: an amendment contractualised recalculates it entirely on the latter. It forms the S-curve of the project, beside the [earned value](valeur-acquise.md) and the actual cost; the schedule variance and the [schedule index](indices.md) compare it to the earned value.
